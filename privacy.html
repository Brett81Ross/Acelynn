Acelynn Pro™ Privacy Policy

Effective: September 21, 2026 · Cactus🌵Byte Studios™

Privacy summary: Acelynn Pro is designed to analyze audio on your device. Cactus🌵Byte Studios does not receive, collect, sell, or share your microphone audio, imported audio files, or locally saved Acelynn analysis data through the current Acelynn Pro service.

Microphone access

Acelynn Pro requests microphone access only when you start live analysis. Microphone input is used to calculate frequency and level information on your device. Acelynn Pro does not upload or transmit the microphone audio to Cactus🌵Byte Studios.

Audio files

If you choose an audio file for analysis, Acelynn Pro processes that file on your device. The source audio file is not uploaded to Cactus🌵Byte Studios. Acelynn may calculate a local cryptographic hash and retain limited source metadata, such as file name, type, and size, to support local analysis and version matching.

Information stored on your device

Acelynn Pro can store analysis results, session snapshots, room or speaker signatures, preferences, limited diagnostic information, source metadata and hashes, and user-created backup data locally on your device. You control exported backup files and any locations or services to which you choose to move them.

Data collection and sharing

The current Acelynn Pro service does not use an Acelynn account, advertising SDK, or analytics service, and Cactus🌵Byte Studios does not collect or share the locally processed audio or analysis information described above through Acelynn Pro.

Permissions

The Google Play version uses microphone permission for live analysis and network access to load the Acelynn Pro web application. The Play version does not request camera, precise or approximate location, or notification permission.

Retention and deletion

Locally saved Acelynn data remains on the device until you clear it through available app/browser storage controls, replace or remove local data, or uninstall/clear the app's storage as applicable. Exported backup files remain wherever you choose to save them until you delete them.

Security

Acelynn Pro minimizes remote data handling by performing its audio analysis locally. No method of software operation or device storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so you should protect access to your device and any backup files you export.

Children

Acelynn Pro is a general-purpose audio analysis tool and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If Acelynn Pro changes how it handles data, this policy and applicable Google Play disclosures will be updated to reflect those changes.

Contact

Questions about this privacy policy can be directed to Cactus🌵Byte Studios through the developer contact information published with Acelynn Pro on Google Play.
